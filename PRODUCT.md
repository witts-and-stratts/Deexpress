# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: greenfield Next.js App Router with TypeScript and CSS modules/global CSS; chosen because the brief explicitly recommends a production React stack and the repository contains no existing framework.

## Users

Individuals and businesses planning international shipment from Europe to destinations across Africa and the Middle East, plus customers checking an existing shipment or asking about vehicle sourcing.

## Product Purpose

DEExpress Logistics is an international logistics and freight-forwarding company that helps customers coordinate air freight, sea freight, vehicle shipping, commercial cargo, personal effects and vehicle sourcing. Success means a visitor understands the offer quickly and can either request a quote or check an existing shipment.

## Positioning

DEExpress makes cross-border logistics easier to navigate by coordinating the route, operational handoffs and customer communication around the cargo and destination.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive with a shipment requirement, origin/destination question, vehicle sourcing need, or shipment reference. They need concise service guidance, honest coverage information and clear next actions without fabricated guarantees or operational data.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities: international air and sea freight from Europe to 26 African countries and supported destinations in the Middle East; vehicle shipping; commercial cargo transportation; personal effects shipping; freight coordination; delivery management; vehicle sourcing and purchasing assistance. Exact countries, contact details, tracking API, legal copy, leadership details, testimonials, offices and guarantees are not supplied and must remain configurable or absent.

## Brand Commitments

Name: DEExpress Logistics. Voice: professional, concise, confident, international, precise and calm. Visual brief: editorial, cinematic, technologically advanced but restrained; concept “control across distance”; navy/royal blue foundation, warm off-white surfaces and controlled orange signal accents; serif editorial display with modern sans-serif UI.

## Evidence on Hand

The supplied master brief is the only content and product evidence. No logos, imagery, approved legal text, customer testimonials, client logos, destination country list, tracking integration or contact details are present in the repository.

## Product Principles

- Make cross-border logistics understandable.
- Keep claims factual and configurable.
- Show the route and the next action clearly.
- Serve both individuals and businesses without forcing one workflow.
- Let operational clarity create trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, with keyboard access, visible focus, semantic structure, labelled form controls, live status messaging and reduced-motion support.
